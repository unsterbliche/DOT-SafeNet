Help

Model components and interpretation of the public v1.0.0 release.

OT-ProfileNet

OT-ProfileNet predicts pAC50 values for 194 human safety targets. Higher pAC50 corresponds to a lower predicted AC50.

Free systemic exposure

PlasmaBindNet-Fu and DoseExpoNet estimate PPB and dose-dependent total Cmax. Free Cmax is calculated from the predicted unbound fraction.

DOT-SafeNet

The clinically fine-tuned five-fold ensemble combines free exposure, 194 target features and their target-exposure products to predict 18 SOC-level ADR scores.

Samples

Meclofenamic acid, Citalopram, Spironolactone and Candesartan cilexetil reproduce the clinical-dose and target-attribution cases used in the manuscript.

Batch Input

Manual prediction accepts name,SMILES lines, SMILES-only lines, pasted CSV-style text, SDF, MOL2, TXT or CSV. New-molecule calculations use the published v1.0.0 code and deposited checkpoints.

Interpretation

Outputs are research signals for prioritization and hypothesis generation. They are not clinical, regulatory, prescribing, or toxicology-study replacement decisions.
